## Store listing: English

**Short description** (max 80 characters)
> Night rides in Torino with verified women drivers. Book, check, share, arrive.

**Full description**
> Wubber is a night-time ride service in Torino where every driver is a woman, checked in person by our team.
>
> HOW IT WORKS
> • Choose your pickup and destination anywhere in Torino, or drop a pin on the map.
> • Our team matches you with a verified female driver and confirms the price.
> • Watch her car come to you on the map.
> • Check her photo, car and plate, then give her your 4-digit PIN. She can only start the ride with it.
>
> SAFETY FIRST
> • Every driver's ID and NCC licence is checked in person.
> • Share your trip live with a friend: they see your ride and where you are until you arrive.
> • One tap to alert the pilot team or call 112.
>
> MADE FOR YOUR NIGHTS OUT
> • Save Home, University and Work for one-tap booking.
> • See your past rides and book them again.
> • English and Italian, light and dark mode.
>
> Wubber is a pilot run by students at ESCP Business School in Torino. Your feedback after each ride helps us decide how to launch.

## App content forms

**Privacy policy:** https://wubber-pi.vercel.app/privacy.html

**Ads:** No, the app has no ads.

**App access:** "All or some functionality is restricted." Give Google a test rider account (email + password) so the reviewer can log in. Create a dedicated one; don't use your own.

**Target audience:** 18 and over.

**Content rating questionnaire:** Category "All other app types". Answer No to violence, sexuality, gambling and similar questions. Yes to "Users can interact or exchange information" (riders and drivers share names and phone numbers) and Yes to "Shares the user's location with other users" (live trip sharing and the driver's car on the map).

**Data safety**

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Why | Optional |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | Yes, with the assigned driver | App functionality | No |
| Email address | Yes | No | Account management | No |
| Phone number | Yes | Yes, with the assigned driver | App functionality | No |
| Precise location | Yes | Yes, with the driver and anyone holding a share link | App functionality | Yes |
| Photos (drivers only) | Yes | Yes, with passengers | App functionality | No for drivers |
| App interactions / in-app feedback | Yes | No | Analytics (pilot research) | Yes |

Also answer: data is encrypted in transit (Yes); users can request deletion (Yes, via Instagram @wubber2027 as stated in the privacy notice).

**Government, health, financial, news apps:** No to all.

## Before you can publish to everyone

New personal Google Play developer accounts must first run a **closed test with at least 12 testers who stay opted in for 14 days in a row**. Only then can you apply for production (public) access. Classmates, interviewees and friends with Android phones count.
